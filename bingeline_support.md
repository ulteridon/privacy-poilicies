## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create an account?

Open Bingeline, choose a display name and a 4–8 digit PIN. Your display name becomes your username, so "Movie Buff" becomes @moviebuff. No email address or phone number is needed. Bingeline shares accounts with the WatchThis website, so you can sign in to either with the same name and PIN.

### Why does my account say it's waiting for approval?

New accounts are reviewed by the editor (our moderator) to keep the community free of spam. Until then you can track titles and build lists, but your profile is hidden from others and you can't yet see the lists and history of people you follow. You don't need to do anything; the editor will approve it as soon as they can.

### I forgot my PIN. How do I get back in?

For your security your PIN is stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can look it up. If you are still signed in on a device, go to Settings and change your PIN there. If you are signed out everywhere, there is currently no way to reset a PIN. Email us with your username if you need help or want the account deleted.

### How do I track a show's episodes?

Open a show and mark the episodes you have watched. Bingeline works out your next episode and shows it in your watch queue. Movies and shows can also get a 1–5 star rating and an emoji reaction.

### How do new-episode reminders work?

When reminders are on, Bingeline sends at most one notification a day, at 10:00 local time, on days when shows you track have a new episode. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and nothing about them is sent to us. If you don't get them, check **iOS Settings → Bingeline → Notifications** and make sure reminders are turned on in the app. Opening the app refreshes the schedule.

### Who can see my lists and watch history?

Your lists, watch history and ratings are visible to approved accounts that follow you and to the editor. Your display name, profile photo, stats and comments are public once your account is approved. To stop someone seeing your activity, open their profile and block them.

### How do I report a comment, profile or list?

Use the **Report** option on the comment, profile or list. Pick a reason and add details if you like. The editor reviews every report and can remove content or suspend accounts.

### Where does the "where to watch" information come from?

Title details, posters and images come from TMDB. Streaming, rental and purchase availability comes from JustWatch through TMDB, and can differ by country or lag behind a service's own catalogue. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

### Is Bingeline free? Do I need to restore purchases?

Yes, Bingeline is free with no ads and no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to restore. Sign in with your name and PIN on a new iPhone and your lists and history come back.

### Does Bingeline access my photos?

Only when you choose to set a profile picture. Bingeline uses Apple's photo picker, so it receives just the photo you pick, removes its location and other metadata, and uploads it as your profile picture. It never scans your library. If iOS shows a Photos prompt, you can change your choice any time in **iOS Settings → Bingeline → Photos**.

## Deleting Your Account and Data

- **In the app:** **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, photo, lists, watch history, ratings, episode progress, follows, blocks and comments are erased.
- **On the web:** use the Delete account page on the WatchThis website with your name and PIN.
- **Can't sign in?** Email asquare938@gmail.com with your username and the subject "Bingeline account deletion", and we will delete it for you.

Deleting the app from your iPhone only removes the copy stored on your phone. It does not delete your account. See our [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=bingeline_privacy_policy.md) for what is deleted and what may remain.
