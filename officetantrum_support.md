## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Office Tantrum work?

Pick a male or female boss, optionally add a face from a photo and a voice clip, choose today's reason (or type your own), and let it out: punch, slap, poke, tickle, throw pies, tomatoes and coffee, or use the weapons from the shop. The boss reacts to every hit until he is defeated, and you earn coins along the way. **Quick play** on the title screen skips the photo and voice steps.

### Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is processed only on your iPhone, using face-detection models that are built into the App. It is never uploaded and never saved; it stays in memory only while the App is open and is used only to put a cartoon face on the 3D character. Please only use your own face, or a face you have permission to use.

### The App says it couldn't find a face. What can I do?

Use a clear, well-lit photo where the face is looking at the camera and isn't covered by sunglasses or hands. JPG, PNG and WebP photos are supported. If no face is found you can still tap **Position it** and place the photo yourself. In a group photo, tap the numbered face you want.

### Why does the App ask for microphone access?

Only when you tap **Record** (to record a short boss voice clip, up to 10 seconds) or **TALK** (so the boss can answer back when you pause). Your voice stays on your iPhone, in memory only, and is never uploaded or saved. If you declined by mistake, turn it on in **iOS Settings > Office Tantrum > Microphone**. You can also pick an audio file instead of recording.

### The boss doesn't answer what I said in Talk mode.

The boss only tries to understand your words when your iPhone can recognise speech fully on the device. Otherwise he answers with a line for today's reason. Make sure TALK is on (the microphone closes when you pause or the fight ends), speak clearly, then pause for a moment so he gets his turn.

### How do coins and coin packs work?

You earn coins by playing, and you can buy optional coin packs. Coins unlock weapons in the shop. Coin packs are consumable Apple In-App Purchases: each one adds coins once, and you can buy it again. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

### I bought coins but they didn't show up.

Close and reopen the App, then open the shop. The App checks for completed purchases when it starts and when the shop opens, and adds any coins that are still missing (each purchase is only counted once). If they still haven't arrived, email us with the date of purchase and the pack you bought (your Apple receipt email is enough, with any payment details removed).

### Can I restore my coins on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

Unfortunately not. Coin packs are consumable purchases, and your coin balance and unlocked weapons are stored only on your iPhone. Apple does not allow consumable purchases to be restored, and we have no account or server to keep a copy. Deleting the App removes your coins.

### How do I get a refund?

In-App Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with the Apple ID you used, and choose the purchase.

### How do I delete my data or reset the game?

There is no account, so nothing is stored on our side. Photos and voice recordings are never saved. To remove everything else (settings, coins, unlocked weapons), delete the App from your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=officetantrum_privacy_policy.md) for details.

## Reporting a Bug

These details help us fix things faster:

- iPhone model (for example, iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings > General > About)
- Office Tantrum version
- What you were doing and what happened
- A screen recording, if possible (please don't send photos of real people)
