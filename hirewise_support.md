## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Hirewise work?

1. Type or paste your resume details: experience, education, skills and achievements.
2. Paste the job description you are applying for.
3. Tap to generate. Hirewise sends both to our server, where an AI model rewrites your resume to match the job, using the keywords and structure that applicant tracking systems (ATS) look for.
4. Review the result and save it as a PDF on your iPhone.

Each generated resume uses one credit. New users get 2 free credits.

### What does "ATS-friendly" mean?

Many employers use applicant tracking systems to scan resumes before a person reads them. Hirewise uses a simple single-column layout, standard section headings and plain text (no tables or images), and works relevant wording from the job description into your resume so it reads well to both software and people. No tool can guarantee an interview, so always check the result before you send it.

### Is my resume sent anywhere?

Yes, but only when you tap to generate. Your resume details and the job description are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), so the AI can write your resume. The app asks for your permission before the first upload. We don't keep your resume text after the request is finished, server logs (which don't include your resume text) are deleted within 30 days, and nothing is sold or used for advertising. Full details are in our [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=hirewise_privacy_policy.md).

Tip: leave out information a resume doesn't need, such as ID numbers or your date of birth.

### Where are my PDFs saved, and how do I send one?

PDFs are created and stored on your iPhone, inside Hirewise. To send one, open it and tap **Share**: you can email it, attach it to a job application, or tap **Save to Files** to keep a copy in the Files app or iCloud Drive.

### Do I need an account?

No. Hirewise has no sign-up or login. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone except the text sent to generate each resume. If you use the form, your contact details (name, email, phone, city and links) are not sent at all; they're added to the PDF on your iPhone.

### What permissions does Hirewise need?

None. Hirewise doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. It only needs an internet connection to generate resumes.

### How do credits work?

You buy credit packs through Apple In-App Purchase, and each generated resume uses one credit. Credits don't expire. Your balance is stored only on your iPhone; nothing about your purchases is sent to our server. If a generation fails because of a problem on our side, the credit is not used. If you think a credit was taken by mistake, email us.

### I bought credits but they didn't appear. Can I restore them?

Credit packs are consumable purchases, so Apple doesn't restore them the way it restores a one-time unlock or subscription. Try this first:

- Make sure you're connected to the internet, then fully close and reopen Hirewise. A purchase that was interrupted is usually completed the next time the app opens.
- If you see a **Restore Purchases** button in the store screen, tap it.

Credits are stored only on your iPhone, so unused credits don't move to a new iPhone and are lost if you delete the app. We don't keep a copy of your balance on our server, so we can't restore it from our side. If you were charged but credits never appeared, email us with your Apple receipt or order ID (from the purchase email Apple sent you, or from [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)) and we'll help, or request a refund from Apple there.

### Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with the Apple ID you used to buy the credits.

### The resume was generated but something is wrong or missing.

The AI only knows what you give it, so add more detail to your resume information (for example results, numbers and tools you used) and generate again. Always read the result before sending it: the AI can occasionally get a detail wrong or phrase something in a way you don't like, and you are responsible for making sure your resume is accurate.

### How do I delete my data?

- **From your iPhone:** go to **Settings → Delete all data** to remove your saved details, job descriptions, generated resumes and their PDFs (your credit balance is kept). Uninstalling Hirewise deletes everything it stored, including your credit balance. PDFs you saved to Files or sent by email need to be deleted there.
- **From our server:** we don't keep your resume text, credits or purchase records, and technical logs (which don't include your resume text) are deleted automatically within 30 days. To have them deleted sooner, email **asquare938@gmail.com** with the subject **Hirewise Data Deletion** and roughly when you used the app, and we'll delete matching records.

Because Hirewise has no accounts, there is no account to delete.

## Reporting a Bug

Including the following helps us fix things faster:

- iPhone model (for example, iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- Hirewise version (shown in the app's settings)
- What you were doing when the problem happened, and what you expected to happen

Please don't email us your full resume unless we ask for it to investigate a problem.
