# Privacy Policy for Bingeline: TV & Movie Tracker

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## Introduction

Bingeline lets you track what you watch, rate titles, keep lists, follow other curators and get a reminder when shows you track have a new episode. Bingeline is the iPhone app of the WatchThis website and uses the same accounts. This policy explains what Bingeline collects, who can see it and how you can delete it.

Bingeline is published on the App Store by Akash Sinha, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). Questions: **asquare938@gmail.com**.

## The Short Version

- We collect only what Bingeline needs to work: the name you choose, your PIN (stored only as a one-way hash), and the lists, ratings, watch progress, follows and comments you add.
- Bingeline is free. No ads, no selling your data, no third-party analytics or tracking.
- Your lists and watch history are visible only to approved accounts that follow you, plus the editor (moderator). Your display name, profile photo, summary stats and comments are public once your account is approved.
- You can delete your account at any time in the app under **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. Your data is erased immediately.

## What We Collect

### Your account

- **Display name.** The name you choose. It also becomes your username, for example "Movie Buff" becomes @moviebuff.
- **PIN.** Your 4–8 digit PIN is never stored as typed. We keep only a salted, one-way hash that can check a PIN but cannot be turned back into it. We count failed attempts so we can temporarily lock an account after repeated wrong PINs.
- **Account status.** When you joined, and whether your account is waiting for approval, approved or suspended.

The Bingeline app does not ask for your email address, phone number or real name.

### What you add

- Lists and the titles in them
- Titles you mark as watched, with any star ratings (1–5) and emoji reactions
- Episode-by-episode progress for shows
- Who you follow and who you block
- Comments you post on titles, and whether you marked them as spoilers
- A profile photo, if you choose one. Only the photo you pick is read and uploaded. We re-encode it, which removes its metadata, including any location it was taken at.

### Reports

If you report a comment, profile or list, we store who sent the report, what it is about, the reason and any details you add, so the editor can review it.

### Technical information

- To keep you signed in, Bingeline stores a sign-in token in the iOS Keychain. Each token is valid for up to 90 days at a time.
- Bingeline keeps a copy of what it has loaded on your phone so it opens quickly. Signing out clears it.
- Our server host, Vercel, processes standard request information (such as IP address, device type and the requests made) to deliver the service and protect it from abuse.
- To limit abuse, such as repeated wrong PINs, mass sign-ups or report spam, we keep the times of recent attempts. Counts per IP address are filed under a one-way scrambled form of the address, never the address itself.
- We do not use analytics tools, advertising identifiers, tracking pixels or any third-party SDK that tracks you.

## How We Use It

- To run Bingeline: showing your profile, lists, stats and badges; sharing your activity with your followers; displaying comments; and suggesting titles based on what is in your lists.
- To keep the community safe: reviewing new accounts, acting on reports, removing content that breaks the rules, and limiting wrong PIN attempts, sign-ups, reports and rapid-fire posting.
- To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

## Who Can See What

- **Everyone, once your account is approved:** your display name and username, profile photo, join date, who you follow and who follows you, your summary stats (time watched, number of movies, shows, episodes, ratings and lists, follower counts, longest streak) and the comments you post on titles.
- **Approved accounts that follow you, and the editor:** your lists, the titles you have watched with your ratings and reactions, your episode progress, and suggestions based on your lists. Following does not need your approval, so block anyone you do not want seeing these. Anyone you block cannot follow you or see any of this.
- **While your account waits for approval:** your profile is hidden from everyone except you and the editor.
- **If your account is suspended:** your profile, lists and history are hidden from everyone except the editor, and your comments are hidden from everyone.
- **Only you:** the list of people you have blocked and your sign-in details. Your PIN itself is never stored, so nobody can look it up, not even us.

The editor is the moderator account marked "Editor". To moderate the community, the editor can see every account's lists and history, approve or suspend accounts and remove content.

## Third-Party Services

- **Vercel** hosts our server and stores account data in Vercel Blob storage in the United States. Account data sits in a private store that can only be read through our server. Profile photos are stored as public images so they can be shown to anyone.
- **TMDB** (The Movie Database) provides movie and TV information, posters and images. Our server asks TMDB about titles (for example title IDs and the searches you type) without any information about you. Posters and images load directly from TMDB's image servers, which see your IP address like any website does. *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.*
- **JustWatch**, through TMDB, provides where each title is available to stream, rent or buy.
- **YouTube and streaming services.** Trailer and "where to watch" links open YouTube or services such as Netflix or Prime Video, whose own privacy policies apply there.
- **Apple** distributes the app through the App Store and delivers notifications on your iPhone.

Bingeline is free and has no in-app purchases. We do not share your personal information with anyone else unless the law requires it or it is needed to protect someone's safety.

Google sign-in, offered on the WatchThis website only, is handled by Supabase. The Bingeline iPhone app does not use Google sign-in or Supabase.

## Notifications and Permissions

- **Notifications (optional).** New-episode reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone: at most one reminder a day, on days when shows you track have a new episode. We do not use a push-notification server, and nothing about your notifications is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.
- **Photos (optional).** Used only when you choose a profile photo. Only the photo you pick is uploaded.

Bingeline does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

## Storage, Security and Retention

- Account data is stored with Vercel in the United States. If you use Bingeline from elsewhere, for example India, your data is transferred to and stored in the US.
- PIN hashes live in separate private records, never in your profile. Sign-in tokens are signed so they cannot be forged. Signing out ends the session on that device, you can sign out of every device at once, and changing your PIN signs out every other device.
- No online service can promise perfect security, but we keep what we store to a minimum and limit who can read it.
- We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Anything you delete yourself (a comment, a list, a rating) is removed straight away. When you upload a new profile photo, your earlier ones are deleted.

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time:

- **In the app:** Profile → Settings → Delete account.
- **On the web:** the Delete account page on the WatchThis website (sign in with your name and PIN), which works even if your account is suspended.
- **By email:** if you cannot sign in, for example you have forgotten your PIN, email asquare938@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Deletion is immediate and permanent, and a deleted account's username can never be registered again. It erases:

- Your profile: display name, username, profile photos and account status
- All your lists and the titles in them
- Your watch history, star ratings and emoji reactions
- Episode progress for every show you track
- Who you follow and who you have blocked, and your name in other people's follow and block lists
- Every comment you have posted
- Your sign-in details: your PIN hash and every device's sign-in session

What may remain:

- Abuse reports stay with the editor as moderation records, with no automatic expiry. Reports you sent are marked as coming from a deleted user; reports about you keep your username. They contain usernames, the reason and what the reporter wrote, not your lists or history.
- A note that your username belonged to a deleted account, and when it was deleted, so the name can never be registered again. It holds nothing else about you.
- Copies of your profile photo cached by networks can take a short while to disappear.

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the local cache and sign-in token, but does not delete your account. Use one of the options above for that.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can see and change almost everything we hold about you directly in Bingeline: delete comments and lists, unmark watched titles, clear ratings, unfollow or block people, and change your PIN. To get a copy of your data, fix something you cannot edit yourself, or ask anything about your privacy, email asquare938@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, data-protection laws may give you further rights, and we will respect them.

## Children's Privacy

Bingeline is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email asquare938@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

When we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app before they take effect.
