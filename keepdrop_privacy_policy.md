# Privacy Policy for KeepDrop: Swipe Photo Cleaner

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

**App:** KeepDrop: Swipe Photo Cleaner (iPhone)
**Developer:** Akash Sinha, individual developer, India
**Contact:** asquare938@gmail.com

## Introduction

KeepDrop ("we," "our," or "us") helps you clean up your photo library. You swipe through your photos and videos by album or by month, choose what to keep and what to delete, review your choices, and then delete the ones you don't want.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the KeepDrop app (the "App") handles and what stays on your iPhone. The short version: **your photos and videos never leave your device through KeepDrop, and we do not collect any data about you.**

By using KeepDrop, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 No Accounts, No Personal Information

KeepDrop has no user accounts. There is no sign-up or login. **We do not collect:**

- Your name, email address or phone number
- Your contacts or location
- Advertising identifiers (KeepDrop does not use the IDFA and does not track you)
- Usage analytics, crash reports or behavioural data

### 1.2 Photos and Videos

KeepDrop needs access to your photo library to show you your photos and videos so you can decide what to keep.

- iOS asks you to allow access. You can choose **Full Access** or **Limited Access** (only the photos you pick). KeepDrop works with either.
- Your photos and videos are read and displayed **only on your device**
- They are **never uploaded** to our servers or to any third party. We have no servers that receive your photos
- KeepDrop does not analyse the content of your photos (faces, places or text) on or off the device
- Album names and photo dates are used only on your device, to group your library by album or by month

If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage", iOS may download a full-size copy of a photo or video from iCloud when KeepDrop displays it. That download is handled by Apple's Photos system, not by us.

### 1.3 Deleting Photos and Videos

- KeepDrop never deletes anything silently. When you confirm a deletion, the App asks iOS to delete the items, and **iOS shows its own confirmation dialog** before anything is removed
- Deleted items go to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days so you can recover them
- If you use iCloud Photos, items deleted on your iPhone are also deleted from iCloud and your other devices, as with any deletion in the Photos app
- We keep no record of what you deleted

### 1.4 Information Stored on Your Device

KeepDrop saves a small amount of information in its own private storage on your iPhone:

- Your keep/delete choices and progress for albums or months you have started, so you can pick up where you left off
- Simple counts, such as how many items you reviewed and how much storage you freed
- Your settings (for example theme) and whether you have finished the intro screens
- Whether you own KeepDrop Pro, so it works offline

This information stays on your device. It refers to photos only by the internal identifiers iOS assigns; it does not contain copies of your photos. It is deleted when you delete the App.

### 1.5 Purchases (KeepDrop Pro)

KeepDrop Pro is an optional **one-time purchase** (non-consumable) made through **Apple In-App Purchase**.

- **Payment is handled entirely by Apple.** We never see your card, Apple ID, name or billing details
- The App talks to Apple's App Store directly through Apple's StoreKit framework, on your iPhone, to show the price, make the purchase and check whether you own Pro (for example after a reinstall or when you tap **Restore Purchases**). **No third-party purchase service is used**, and we run no server that receives purchase information
- Whether you own Pro is remembered on your iPhone so it works offline
- Apple shares sales reports with us in App Store Connect under Apple's own policy; these do not identify you to us

### 1.6 No Ads and No Analytics

KeepDrop does not show ads and does not include any advertising or analytics SDK. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Apple may collect crash reports and app usage data if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**. Apple shares this with us only in aggregated, anonymous form, under Apple's own privacy policy.

## 3. Third Parties

We do **not** sell, rent or trade any information. KeepDrop contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or purchase-management SDKs. The only third party involved is:

- **Apple** — App Store distribution, In-App Purchase payment (through StoreKit), and the Photos system that stores and deletes your photos. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

If you tap a link in the App (Privacy Policy, Support or Terms of Use), the page opens in a browser and is served by that website (GitHub Pages or Apple) under its own policy. The App itself sends nothing with it.

We may disclose information if required by law. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, in practice there is nothing for us to disclose.

## 4. Storage and Retention

- Photos and videos stay in your Photos library, under your control
- App data (progress, stats, settings, Pro status) stays on your iPhone until you delete the App
- Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple's policy. We keep no purchase records of our own

---

## 5. Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

- **Photo access:** change it at any time in **Settings → KeepDrop → Photos** (Full Access, Limited Access or None). Without access, KeepDrop cannot show your library
- **App data:** deleting KeepDrop from your iPhone removes all of its saved progress, stats and settings. Your photos are not affected
- **Recover deleted photos:** open the Photos app → Albums → **Recently Deleted** (within about 30 days)
- **Purchase records:** we hold none. Your purchase history is kept by Apple; see [Apple's privacy page](https://privacy.apple.com) to manage data Apple holds about you
- Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to access, correct or delete data about you. Email us and we will help

## 6. Children's Privacy

KeepDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.
