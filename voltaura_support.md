## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make the animation play when I plug in my iPhone?

iOS doesn't let apps start on their own when a charger is connected, so Voltaura uses a Shortcuts automation:

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **Automation**.
2. Tap **+** (or **New Automation**) and choose **Charger**.
3. Select **Is Connected**, and choose **Run Immediately** so it doesn't ask each time.
4. Add the action **Open App** and pick **Voltaura**.
5. Tap **Done**.

Now plug in your phone. Voltaura opens and plays your selected animation. Voltaura's in-app guide walks you through the same steps.

### The animation doesn't start when I plug in. What should I check?

- Open Shortcuts → Automation and make sure the **Charger → Is Connected** automation is turned on.
- Make sure it's set to **Run Immediately**; otherwise iOS shows a notification you have to tap.
- Check that the automation's **Open App** action points to Voltaura (reinstalling the app can break this link; edit the action and pick Voltaura again).
- If your iPhone is locked, iOS may open the app only after you unlock it. This is an iOS limitation.

### How do I stop the animation from showing?

Turn off or delete the Charger automation in Shortcuts → Automation. Voltaura never runs in the background, so once the automation is off, the app will only open when you tap it.

### How do I change the animation?

Open Voltaura, browse the animations, tap one to preview it, and select it. The next time your charger is connected, the new animation plays.

### Can I use my own photo as the background?

Yes. In the app's settings, choose a background photo. Voltaura uses Apple's photo picker, so it doesn't need access to your whole library; it only receives the one photo you pick. The photo stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded. You can remove it from the same setting at any time.

### Is the battery percentage accurate?

Voltaura shows the battery level iOS reports to apps. iOS may round this value, so it can differ by a few percent from the number in your status bar.

### What do I get with Voltaura Pro?

Voltaura Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks all Pro animations and features. Voltaura has no ads, with or without Pro.

### How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Then open Voltaura's settings and tap **Restore Purchases**. Pro unlocks again at no extra cost.

### Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

### Does Voltaura collect my data? How do I delete it?

No. Voltaura has no account, no ads, and no analytics, and it doesn't send your data anywhere. Your settings and background photo are stored only on your iPhone. To delete everything, delete the app. Because there is no account and nothing is stored on our side, there is no account deletion request to make. See the [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=voltaura_privacy_policy.md) for details.

## Reporting a Bug

Including the following helps us fix things faster:

- Device model (for example, iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- Voltaura version (shown in the app's settings)
- What you were doing when the issue happened
- What you expected vs. what actually happened
- A screen recording, if possible
