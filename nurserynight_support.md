## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play?

Every night a closet monster creeps out and walks the halls of the house. During the short prep time, pick a room and claim it. Your baby naps in a cradle that earns coins; spend them to upgrade your door and cradle and to build cannons. The nannies defend the rooms next door, and the monster always goes for the weakest door. Survive until the monster is beaten, or be the last one standing, to win the night.

### What game modes are there?

- **Nursery Night** – the classic night as the baby.
- **Blitz Night** – a shorter prep, a tougher monster, and everything runs faster.
- **Be the Monster** – switch sides: sneak past the nannies' cannons and break every door before dawn.

Each room also rolls random quirks every night (a toy chest, a sturdy door, a drafty room and more), so tap a room during prep to see what you'd be getting.

### Is the game free? Are there ads or purchases?

Yes, it's free. Version 1.0 has **no ads and no in-app purchases**. The coins and moonstones you spend in the Shop, the toy Draw and on skins are earned by playing and the daily gift; they can't be bought with real money.

### Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything is built into the app, so you can play fully offline, including on a plane.

### Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone only. There is no account or cloud save in version 1.0, so progress does not sync between devices. It may come back if you restore your new iPhone from a full backup of your old one, but we can't guarantee that.

### I deleted the app and lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Unfortunately not. We never receive your save data, so we have no copy to restore. Deleting the app removes the save file permanently.

### How do I start over?

Open **Settings** in the game and tap **Reset all progress**. This erases your coins, toys and records, but keeps your language, sound and graphics choices.

### The game feels slow or my phone gets warm.

Open **Settings → Graphics** and pick **Low** or **Medium**. Lower settings run smoother and save battery. **Auto** picks a level based on your device. The game restarts briefly to apply a new graphics setting.

### How do I change the language or turn off sound?

Open **Settings** in the game. You can choose English, Español or हिन्दी, and switch sound on or off. You can also tap the speaker button on the home screen to mute.

### Does the game ask for any permissions or collect my data?

No. Nursery Night doesn't ask for any permissions and doesn't collect any personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=nurserynight_privacy_policy.md) for details.

### How do I delete my data?

There is no account, and nothing is stored on our side. To delete all of your game data, use **Settings → Reset all progress**, or simply delete the app from your iPhone.

## Reporting a Bug

Including the following helps us fix things faster:

- Device model (for example, iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- Nursery Night version (shown on the App Store page or in iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Nursery Night)
- Which game mode you were playing, and what happened vs. what you expected
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
