## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does KeepDrop work?

Pick an album or a month, then swipe through your photos and videos one at a time: **swipe right to keep, swipe left to delete**. When you finish, KeepDrop shows you everything you marked for deletion so you can review it and change your mind. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and iOS asks you to confirm once more.

### I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for about 30 days. Open the **Photos** app → **Albums** → **Recently Deleted**, select the item and tap **Recover**. KeepDrop never bypasses Recently Deleted. After iOS empties it (or if you empty it yourself), the item cannot be recovered, so please keep backups of anything important.

### Why does iOS ask me again before deleting?

That is iOS's own safety check. Apps can't delete photos without your approval, so after you confirm in KeepDrop, iOS shows a dialog listing how many items will be deleted. Tap **Delete** to go ahead or **Don't Allow** to cancel. If you cancel, nothing is removed.

### I use iCloud Photos. What happens when I delete?

Deleting a photo on your iPhone also deletes it from iCloud Photos and your other Apple devices, just like deleting it in the Photos app. It still goes to Recently Deleted first. If a photo is stored only in iCloud ("Optimize iPhone Storage"), it may take a moment to load while iOS downloads it.

### KeepDrop only shows some of my photos

You probably gave KeepDrop **Limited Access**. Go to **Settings → KeepDrop → Photos** and choose **Full Access**, or tap **Edit Selected Photos** to add more. If you chose **None**, KeepDrop can't show your library until you allow access there.

### Does KeepDrop upload my photos?

No. Your photos and videos are shown and handled entirely on your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else. KeepDrop has no ads and no analytics. See our [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=keepdrop_privacy_policy.md).

### Can I stop in the middle of an album and come back later?

Yes. KeepDrop remembers your progress and your keep/delete choices for each album or month, so you can pick up where you left off.

### What is KeepDrop Pro?

KeepDrop Pro is a **one-time purchase** (not a subscription) that unlocks the extra features listed on the Pro screen in the app. You pay once through Apple and keep it on every iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID.

### How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

Open the Pro screen (or Settings in the app) and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro. If restore doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase (from your Apple receipt email) and we'll help.

### Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so we can't issue refunds directly. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase and choose KeepDrop Pro.

### How do I delete my data?

KeepDrop has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. All of the app's data (progress, stats and settings) is stored on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. Your photos are not affected. Purchases are made and verified directly with Apple through the App Store, so we hold no purchase records about you; Apple keeps your purchase history under its own privacy policy.

## Reporting a Bug

Including the following helps us fix things faster:

- iPhone model (for example iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- KeepDrop version (shown in the app's Settings)
- What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
