## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I put my name and photo on the posters?

Open the **Profile** tab, tap **Choose Photo** (or **Change Photo**), type your display name, and tap **Save Changes**. You can also tap the photo or name on any poster to change them. Every design updates straight away. Patra keeps up to 12 of your photos so you can switch between them.

### How do I share a poster?

Tap **Share** under any design. Your phone's share sheet opens and you pick where it goes: WhatsApp, Instagram, or **Save Image** to keep it in your Photos. Patra never posts anything for you.

### What does Patra Pro include, and how do I restore it?

Patra Pro removes the watermark from every design you share. To restore it on a new phone, or after reinstalling, open **Profile → Patra Pro** and tap **Restore**.

- **iPhone:** you must be signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
- **Android:** you must be signed in to Google Play with the same Google account.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Deleting the app does not cancel it. Subscriptions are managed by the store:

- **iPhone:** Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Patra
- **Android:** Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Patra

You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

### How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

- **iPhone:** request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Android:** see Google Play's [refund help page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)

### What is AI Create, and how do credits work?

AI Create makes a brand-new poster from your description and any pictures you attach (your photo, a logo, a product). Each picture uses credits: 1 for Standard, 4 for HD. New installs get a few free credits, and you can buy more credit packs as one-time purchases in the app. If a picture fails, the credits are returned.

AI Create is being switched on gradually, so it may say it is coming soon on your phone.

When you tap Create, your description and attached pictures are sent to our server and to Google Gemini to draw the picture. Your other photos and Profile details are not sent. See our [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=mystyle_privacy_policy.md) for details.

### Templates aren't loading or look old

Patra downloads new designs when it starts and keeps a copy for offline use.

- Check that you're connected to the internet
- Fully close Patra and open it again; new content is fetched on launch
- Content we publish can take a few minutes to appear

### Patra can't find my photo

Patra uses the system photo picker, which shows the photos saved on your phone.

- **iPhone:** no Photos permission is needed; Apple's picker shows your whole library and hands Patra only the photo you tap. Photos that are only in iCloud may need a moment to download, so check your internet connection.
- **Android:** no permission is needed. If a photo is missing, make sure it is saved on the phone and not only in a cloud album.

Patra only gets the photo you pick; it never scans your library. It never asks for the camera, microphone or your location.

### Does Patra upload my photos?

No. Your name, photos and Profile answers stay on your phone, and posters are made on your phone. A photo only leaves your phone if you share a poster yourself or attach it to an AI Create request.

### How do I delete my data?

- **Photos:** remove any saved photo in the **Profile** tab.
- **AI creations:** long-press a picture under *Your creations*, then tap **Delete**.
- **Everything on your phone:** uninstall Patra. This deletes your name, photos, answers, AI creations and downloaded designs. Your own gallery is not touched.
- **Purchase and AI credit records:** Patra has no accounts. Email asquare938@gmail.com with the subject "Patra data deletion" and include your Apple order ID (from your App Store receipt email) or Google Play order number. We will delete your RevenueCat purchase record and any AI credit balance within 30 days and confirm by email. Any unused credits are lost when the record is deleted.
