## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Unvarnished work?

Paste something you've written (a resume, a pitch, a dating bio, an essay, a cover letter) or describe a plan or idea, then tap to get feedback. Unvarnished returns blunt, structured feedback: what is not working, why, what to change, and a score. You can reply in the same conversation to ask follow-up questions or paste a revised version.

### Why is the feedback so harsh?

That's the point. Unvarnished is built to skip the compliments and polite padding and tell you plainly what's weak, so you can fix it before a recruiter, investor, or match sees it. It aims to be direct, not cruel: every criticism should come with a reason and a way to improve. If a piece of feedback seems wrong, push back with your reasoning in the conversation; it will change its view when you give it a real argument, not just because you disagree.

### Is the feedback always right?

No. Feedback is generated by an AI model and can be mistaken, miss context, or misjudge your audience. Treat it as a sharp second opinion, not a final verdict, and don't rely on it for legal, medical, or financial decisions.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. The App creates an anonymous ID on your device to count free messages and recognise Pro.

### What does Pro include, and how many free messages do I get?

Free users get a limited number of feedback messages; the current limit is shown in the App. Unvarnished Pro (monthly or yearly) raises that limit so you can keep revising. Prices are shown on the Pro screen in your local currency before you buy.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open the App, go to **Settings** (or the Pro screen), and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. If Pro still doesn't unlock, email us with the support ID shown in Settings.

### How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Unvarnished**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. For refunds, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase; refunds are decided by Apple.

### Where is my text sent? Is it stored?

Your text goes to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to write the feedback. The App asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent. Our server does not keep a copy after the request finishes, and your submissions are not used to train AI models. Your conversations are saved only on your iPhone. We'd still recommend removing phone numbers, addresses, and other sensitive details before pasting. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=unvarnished_privacy_policy.md).

### What permissions does the App need?

Only an internet connection. Unvarnished doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

### How do I delete my conversations or my data?

- Delete a single conversation inside the App, or delete the App to remove all conversations and the anonymous ID from your iPhone.
- To delete the message count and subscription records tied to your anonymous ID on our side, email us at asquare938@gmail.com with the subject **Unvarnished Data Deletion** and include the support ID from the App's Settings screen. We'll confirm once it's done.

### I get an error or the feedback never arrives

- Check your internet connection and try again
- Very long text may need to be shortened; try sending one section at a time
- If you're a free user, you may have reached your message limit; the App will tell you if so
- Update to the latest version from the App Store

If it keeps happening, email us with the details below.

## Reporting a Bug

These details help us fix problems faster:

- iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- Unvarnished version (shown in the App's Settings screen)
- The support ID from Settings (only if the issue is about Pro or message limits)
- What you were doing and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible; please blur anything private
