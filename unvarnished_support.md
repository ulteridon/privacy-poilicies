## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Unvarnished work?

Pick what you're checking (resume bullets, a startup pitch, a dating bio, or a cover letter or About section), paste your text, then tap to get feedback. Unvarnished returns blunt, structured feedback: what is not working, why, what to change, and a score. If you disagree, you can push back once with your reasoning and get a reconsidered verdict. To check a revised version, paste it as a new verdict.

### Why is the feedback so harsh?

That's the point. Unvarnished is built to skip the compliments and polite padding and tell you plainly what's weak, so you can fix it before a recruiter, investor, or match sees it. It aims to be direct, not cruel: every criticism should come with a reason and a way to improve. If a piece of feedback seems wrong, push back with your reasoning; it will change its view when you give it a real argument, not just because you disagree.

### Is the feedback always right?

No. Feedback is generated by an AI model and can be mistaken, miss context, or misjudge your audience. Treat it as a sharp second opinion, not a final verdict, and don't rely on it for legal, medical, or financial decisions.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. The App creates a random anonymous ID (shown as the Support ID in Settings) that our server uses only to rate-limit requests and prevent abuse. Your daily verdict count and your Pro status are checked on your iPhone.

### What does Pro include, and how many free messages do I get?

Free users get a limited number of verdicts per day; the current limit is shown in Settings. Unvarnished Pro (monthly or yearly) raises the daily limit so you can keep revising. Daily limits reset at midnight on your iPhone. Prices are shown on the Pro screen in your local currency before you buy.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open the App, go to **Settings** (or the Pro screen), and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Purchases are handled by Apple, and the App checks your Pro status from your Apple purchases on the iPhone itself, so we can't see or change it from our side. If Pro still doesn't unlock, check that the subscription is active in iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**, then email us and we'll help you troubleshoot.

### How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Unvarnished**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. For refunds, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase; refunds are decided by Apple.

### Where is my text sent? Is it stored?

Your text goes to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to write the feedback. The App asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent. Our server does not keep a copy after the request finishes, and your submissions are not used to train AI models. Your conversations are saved only on your iPhone. We'd still recommend removing phone numbers, addresses, and other sensitive details before pasting. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](policy.html?file=unvarnished_privacy_policy.md).

### What permissions does the App need?

Only an internet connection. Unvarnished doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

### How do I delete my conversations or my data?

- Delete a single conversation inside the App, or delete the App to remove all conversations and the anonymous ID from your iPhone.
- Our server doesn't keep your submissions, message counts, or purchase records, so there is nothing tied to your anonymous ID stored on our side. Purchase records are kept by Apple. If you have a question or request about your data, email us at asquare938@gmail.com with the subject **Unvarnished Data Deletion** and include the Support ID from the App's Settings screen.

### I get an error or the feedback never arrives

- Check your internet connection and try again
- Very long text may need to be shortened; try sending one section at a time
- You may have reached your daily verdict limit; the App will tell you if so
- If you send many requests in a short time, the server may ask you to wait a minute before trying again
- Update to the latest version from the App Store

If it keeps happening, email us with the details below.

## Reporting a Bug

These details help us fix problems faster:

- iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- Unvarnished version (shown in the App's Settings screen)
- The Support ID from Settings (only if the issue is about limits or errors)
- What you were doing and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible; please blur anything private
